Reading: with plain deliveries the two settings drive the same time (differences of a few tenths of a percent, either way);
preserving clusters is 1.7-4x faster. With priorities and weights live, letting ALNS break clusters gives a LOWER cost but a
9-15 % LONGER drive (it spends driving time on the priority / load terms). Decision: keep ROUTE_ALNS_PRESERVE_CLUSTERS=true.
